## Disagreement protocol

When two reputable sources disagree by >25% on a market figure:

1. Surface **both** with citations + dates.
2. State which you'd weight more and why (recency, methodology, primary-vs-secondary).
3. Mark the claim `Estimate (low confidence)` until a third source corroborates.

## Staleness threshold

Regulatory and competitor claims older than 18 months are flagged as stale. Recommend re-verification before any external deliverable.
