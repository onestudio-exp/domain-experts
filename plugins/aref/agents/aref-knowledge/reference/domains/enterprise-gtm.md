# Enterprise GTM — Anchor-Client Sales Motion

## Summary

The Bilt model and the Amos model share a sales motion: identify large captive consumer ecosystems (rental properties, banked customers, supermarket members), convince the anchor to embed the loyalty platform inside their existing app, share the value with the anchor. This is enterprise SaaS, not consumer growth.

## Sales-cycle baseline

Amos / DAMAC: **9 months** from commercial agreement to live integration. Decompose:

| Stage | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| Initial pitch → commercial alignment | 1–2 months |
| Legal review (MSA, DPA, IP, exclusivity) | 1–2 months |
| Compliance & regulatory sign-off | 1–2 months |
| Technical integration & UAT | 2–3 months |
| Internal pilot & go-live | 1 month |

This is the realistic baseline. Targets shorter than 6 months are unrealistic for a real anchor; longer than 12 months indicates the deal is stalling.

## MEDDPICC for embedded loyalty

- **Metrics** — measurable uplift in anchor's primary KPI (residents transacting, banked customer retention, etc.).
- **Economic buyer** — typically the CMO, Head of Digital, or Head of Customer for the anchor.
- **Decision criteria** — regulatory clearance, data-sovereignty, integration burden, white-label brand control.
- **Decision process** — product, legal, compliance, security, IT, plus business sponsor.
- **Paper process** — MSA + DPA + Statement of Work; banks add risk-management addendum.
- **Identify pain** — anchor's existing loyalty either non-existent or underperforming; merchant network sub-scale to do alone.
- **Champion** — the digital / loyalty / customer lead inside the anchor.
- **Competition** — internal build, alternative loyalty platforms, doing nothing.

## Commercial structure (Amos baseline)

- Setup fee: $50K
- Monthly SaaS guarantee: $10K
- Take rate on transacted volume: separate, transaction-fee structure
- Term: 3 years typical, 5 years for banks

## Land-and-expand

The first anchor is the regulatory and integration template. Subsequent anchors:

- Reuse the regulatory clearance.
- Reuse the integration patterns.
- Reuse the merchant network supply.
- Reuse the SaaS pricing benchmark.

This is the lever: anchor #5's onboarding cost is materially below anchor #1.

## Multi-anchor distribution arithmetic

Total Addressable Embedded Reach (TAER) = sum of contracted anchor user bases. For Amos with DAMAC + the 6-deal pipeline, TAER = ~3M users. This is the headline metric versus a typical consumer-app DAU number.

## Common mistakes

- Underpricing the SaaS floor to "make the deal happen". Floor revenue is the only predictable line; transaction take is volume-dependent.
- Accepting category exclusivity. Limits supply-side options later.
- Failing to spec the data-sovereignty terms early. Banking anchors typically require in-region hosting (UAE / KSA).
