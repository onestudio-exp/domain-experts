# Amos Merchant Network

## Two-layer supply

Amos's merchant supply has two layers as of March 2026:

| Layer | Count | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct merchants** | 170+ outlets signed | Direct Amos sales + partnership signing |
| **Managed merchants via PrioHub** | 900+ attractions | Emirates Group JV partnership |

## Direct merchants (the primary growth path)

- 55 merchants signed directly to the rewards network as of the March 2026 update.
- Active conversations include Pizza Express (14 UAE outlets, signed up the week before the update).
- Merchant participation agreements are standardised.
- Onboarding is streamlined; video-based training materials for merchant staff are complete.
- This is now the primary path to merchant growth.

## PrioHub partnership

Amos's original primary merchant content layer was expected from the **Doodle joint venture**, which did not deliver at the scale required. Amos pivoted to **PrioHub** — a JV within the Emirates Group — executed January 2026, providing immediate access to 900+ attractions across the UAE.

The PrioHub integration:

- Was unplanned and outside the original technical roadmap.
- Required significant design work (PrioHub operates a fundamentally different booking, payment, and UX flow than the Amos platform).
- Was built as a standalone Progressive Web App so the core platform is insulated from supply-partner changes.
- Implementation is underway as of March 2026.

## Launch composition

The initial launch focuses on approximately **180–200 UAE offers** (in addition to the 170+ directly contracted outlets) meeting the **minimum 12% margin threshold**. The remaining 900+ PrioHub attractions are being onboarded in phases during the post-launch period.

## Margin discipline

The 12% minimum-merchant-margin floor means below-margin merchants are deferred or excluded. This discipline:

- Protects unit economics.
- Risks under-supply if applied too rigidly to lower-margin categories that drive frequency (groceries, fuel).

## Operational profile

- Direct sales team needs to be recruited / scaled per the March 2026 stakeholder update — this is one of the immediate priorities tied to the seed round closing.
- AI-powered support layer (see [ai-support-economics.md](ai-support-economics.md)) is the leverage point for scaling the network without proportional headcount.
