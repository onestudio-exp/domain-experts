# Amos Network — Overview

## What it is

Amos Network is **embedded loyalty infrastructure** for the UAE / GCC. It deploys inside the apps of large enterprise clients (real estate developers, banks, retailers, mobility operators), reaching consumers where they already are rather than competing for app downloads.

The model is **B2B2C cashback**: anchor enterprises license the platform; consumers earn cashback on transactions across a participating merchant network; merchants fund the cashback from their margin.

## Core proposition

"We are not a consumer app competing for downloads. We deploy inside the apps of the UAE's largest real estate developers, banks, and retailers — reaching residents where they already live." (Investor Brief, March 2026)

## Business model

### Revenue streams

1. **SaaS fees** from enterprise anchor clients (DAMAC: $50K setup + $10K/mo guarantee)
2. **Take rate** on every cashback-eligible transaction — 10%+ average of merchant margin, no interchange dependency
3. **Future:** data analytics, premium tiers, GCC expansion

### Cost structure that works

- Zero consumer acquisition cost — distribution via partner apps
- AI-powered merchant support at ~7x lower cost than industry headcount baseline
- No CBUAE licence required (operates through Network International's licensed PSP infrastructure)

## Market context

- Middle East loyalty market: $3.0B today, projected $5.6B by 2030 (12.8% CAGR)
- UAE shifting toward embedded loyalty inside high-frequency residential and retail transactions
- Standalone loyalty apps are losing relevance as ecosystem-led models consolidate
