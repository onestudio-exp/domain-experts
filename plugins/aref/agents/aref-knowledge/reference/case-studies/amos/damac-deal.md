# Amos × DAMAC

## Why DAMAC is the anchor

DAMAC Properties is the UAE's largest private luxury real estate developer:

- $2.4B revenue business founded in Dubai 2002
- Operations in 15 global cities (London, Miami, Toronto, Riyadh)
- 65,000+ homes delivered
- $18.3B forward sales backlog (Dec 2024)
- Front-of-shirt sponsor for Chelsea Football Club
- Privately held by founder Hussain Sajwani
- Moody's Ba1 rated

Commercial momentum: November 2024 launch of DAMAC Islands generated AED 10B in sales with 3,100 units sold in a single day.

## Commercial terms

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Contract Signed | 7 December 2025 (MSA executed) |
| Setup fee | $50,000 |
| Monthly SaaS guarantee | $10,000 |
| User reach | 180,000+ residents across 75,000 households |
| Integration target | DAMAC Living (resident-facing mobile app) |
| UAT handover | 10 March 2026 |
| Go-live target | Q1 2026 |

## Sales cycle (the 9-month baseline)

The Amos / DAMAC deal **defined** the realistic enterprise sales cycle: nine months from initial commercial agreement to signed MSA. This baseline is now applied to all subsequent enterprise pipeline forecasting.

## Why DAMAC matters beyond the contract

> "DAMAC is not simply the first client. It is the proof of concept for the entire enterprise model. Winning a contract with a business of this scale and brand standing, getting through their legal, compliance, and technical review process, and delivering a working integration validates every core assumption underpinning AMOS's go-to-market strategy." (Stakeholder Update, March 2026)

Specifically, DAMAC validates:

1. The sales motion (long but completable)
2. The legal and compliance posture (acceptable to a Tier-1 enterprise)
3. The technical integration model (deliverable on schedule)
4. The commercial model (anchor will pay setup + monthly guarantee + transactional revenue share)
5. The regulatory framework (anchor accepts the NI / CBUAE clearance)

## Distribution arithmetic

180,000 residents × ~70% engagement rate × $10/month average earned cashback = ~$15M annualised reward flow against ~$1.5M operator take. This is a single anchor.
