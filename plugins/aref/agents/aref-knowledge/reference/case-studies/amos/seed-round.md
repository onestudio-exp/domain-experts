# Amos — Seed Round

## Terms

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Round size | $2,000,000 |
| Pre-money valuation | $20,000,000 |
| Post-money valuation | $22,000,000 |
| Stage label | Seed |
| Targeted closing | Open as of March 2026 |

## Use of proceeds

Per the March 2026 stakeholder update:

1. Complete the DAMAC build and launch.
2. Finalise FAB Bank (PayIT) MSA and begin technical integration, including Alibaba Cloud hosting migration.
3. Bring RAKEZ + Union Coop live, targeting May 2026.
4. Continue merchant network expansion ahead of DAMAC go-live.
5. Recruit a dedicated merchant recruitment sales team.
6. Extend runway.

## Cash position context

> "The original runway was designed to carry AMOS to October 2025. Through disciplined cost management this has been extended well beyond that point, but the combination of longer enterprise sales cycles and a more demanding build programme has placed real pressure on resources." — *Stakeholder Update, March 2026*

The seed round is described as the bridge to the next phase of growth.

## The Amos pitch in one sentence

> "Bilt Rewards turned rent into loyalty. It went from a $350M seed valuation in 2021 to $10.8 billion today. AMOS is doing the same for the GCC, embedded inside DAMAC's 180,000-resident ecosystem, with regulatory clearance confirmed and an enterprise pipeline touching 3 million users." — *Investor Brief, March 2026*

## Strengths of the position

- Anchor signed and integrated (vs Bilt's seed where Wells Fargo had not yet engaged)
- Regulatory clearance documented
- Operational platform with merchant supply
- AI-driven cost-to-serve disruption operationalised
- 6 enterprise deals in pipeline = ~3M users at zero CAC

## Things investors should ask

- What is the conversion rate from MSA stage to live (industry data: ~70%)?
- What is the operating-cost runway extension from $2M at current burn?
- What happens to the comparables-based valuation if Bilt undergoes a multiple compression in the next 12 months?
- KSA / Egypt expansion timeline and capital cost?
