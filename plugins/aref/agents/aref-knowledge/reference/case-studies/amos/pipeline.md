# Amos Enterprise Pipeline (March 2026)

## Status

Beyond DAMAC, six additional enterprise partnerships in various stages of commercial and technical progression. These are not expressions of interest — they represent documented internal approvals, active compliance processes, and drafted contracts.

**Combined addressable user base: 3M+ users acquired through enterprise integration, not marketing spend = zero CAC.**

## Pipeline table

| Enterprise Partner | User Reach | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DAMAC Properties** (UAE's largest independent property developer) | 180,000+ residents | MSA SIGNED, IN BUILD | UAT 10 March 2026, go-live Q1 2026 |
| **Union Coop** (UAE's largest co-op supermarket) | 500,000+ members | MSA STAGE | April 2026 launch targeted |
| **FAB Bank** (UAE's largest retail bank) | 550,000+ users | MSA STAGE | Digital wallet platform; UAE-hosted infra required (Alibaba Cloud migration in planning) |
| **RAKEZ** (UAE's largest free-zone licence issuer) | 50,000+ licence holders | MSA STAGE | Cashback applicable against licence renewals + visa costs — demonstrates vertical flexibility |
| **ADCB** (UAE's 3rd-largest retail bank) | 1.5M customers | ADVANCED | MSA already drafted |
| **Sharaf DG** (UAE's largest offline electronics retailer) | 1M retail customers | ADVANCED | MSA already drafted |
| **DNATA** (UAE's largest travel operator) | 800,000 travel customers | ADVANCED | MSA already drafted |
| **African Eastern** (UAE's 2nd-largest drinks distributor) | 1M retail customers | MOU SIGNED | Awaiting A&E in-house loyalty system rollout for Amos to complement |

## Significance

- Sum of MSA-stage and Advanced-stage enterprises represents 5.4M users.
- Sum of all pipeline (incl. MOU) is ~6M users; the "3M+" headline figure is the deduplicated, weighted-by-likelihood number.
- Distribution-via-anchor at this scale would cost a B2C app tens of millions in CAC.

## Vertical flexibility demonstrated

- **Real estate** (DAMAC) — service charges + community spend
- **Retail / supermarket** (Union Coop) — grocery + repeat spend
- **Banking** (FAB, ADCB) — broad transaction base, requires UAE-hosted infrastructure
- **Government / freezone** (RAKEZ) — licence renewal, visa cost as redemption category
- **Electronics** (Sharaf DG) — single-anchor merchant + customer base
- **Travel** (DNATA) — high-value, lower-frequency category
- **Drinks distribution** (African Eastern) — partnership-complementary mode

## Operational implication

Each MSA-stage deal is approximately the 9-month baseline from agreement to live. Live enterprise count is therefore expected to expand from 1 (DAMAC) to ~4 by end Q3 2026 if all MSA-stage deals progress on the baseline.
