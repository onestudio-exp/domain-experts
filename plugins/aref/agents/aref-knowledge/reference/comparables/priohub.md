# PrioHub

## Tier classification: **n/a — supply partner, not a comparable**

PrioHub is intentionally filed under `comparables/` for ecosystem-mapping reasons but **does not classify as Direct / Indirect / Substitute** under Aref's competitor schema. Reasoning:

- **Relationship type:** Supply-side partner to Amos (provides 900+ attractions / experiences inventory). Not a competitor in any market.
- **Schema rule:** *"You classify every **comparable / competitor** you mention into exactly one tier"* — PrioHub is neither, so the tier rule does not apply.
- **What this file is for:** documenting **the supply infrastructure layer that exists in MENA**, so Aref can reason about Amos's merchant-supply economics and dependency exposure.

When Aref is asked "list Amos's competitors" or "compare Amos to <comparable>", **PrioHub must NOT be returned as a tier-labelled comparable**. It should be referenced as supply-partner context only (in network-mapping, dependency analysis, or merchant-margin reasoning).

## Snapshot

- **Market:** UAE
- **Model:** Joint venture within the Emirates Group; supply-side platform of attractions and experiences
- **Scale:** 900+ attractions across UAE
- **Status:** Privately held, JV structure; supply partner to loyalty platforms

## Why PrioHub matters for Amos

PrioHub is Amos's secondary merchant supply partner, brought in (per the March 2026 stakeholder update) after Doodle, the original JV intended to provide the primary merchant content layer, did not deliver at the scale required.

Key implications:

- **900+ attractions** is a meaningful supply boost beyond Amos's 170+ direct outlets.
- The integration is technically demanding (different booking, payment, UX flow than Amos's core platform).
- Built as a standalone Progressive Web App so Amos's core platform is insulated from supply-partner changes.

## Comparable role

PrioHub is **not a peer** to Amos — it is a supply partner. The comparable lens is "the kind of supply infrastructure that exists in MENA". For investors and competitors, the takeaway is:

1. UAE attraction supply is consolidated through a few providers (PrioHub, Tixr, native travel marketplaces).
2. A loyalty operator without its own supply must contract for it; the contract terms shape the operator's margin.
3. PrioHub's role validates that there is supply for which an embedded loyalty operator can negotiate a take rate.
