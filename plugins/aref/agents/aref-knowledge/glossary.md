# Glossary

**Anchor client** — A large enterprise (developer, bank, retailer, mobility operator) whose existing app embeds the loyalty platform, providing distribution at zero CAC. Bilt's Wells Fargo + 4M apartment units; Amos's DAMAC.

**B2B2C** — Operator sells to a business (B), which delivers the service to consumers (C). Loyalty platforms typically sell SaaS + take rate to anchors and merchants while the consumer never directly contracts with the loyalty operator.

**Breakage** — Unredeemed loyalty value (points, cashback) that expires or is forgotten. A revenue line for closed-loop programs; a liability reduction for accounting.

**Burn** — The redemption side of a points / cashback ledger. "Burn rate" tracks redemption velocity.

**CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost. For embedded loyalty inside an anchor app, near-zero per consumer.

**Cashback matrix** — A grid of cashback rates by merchant × category × tier (or by transaction value bands).

**Closed-loop** — Loyalty value redeemable only inside the operator's network (Amos, Starbucks Rewards). Contrast with open-loop (transferable to multiple parties).

**Cost-to-serve** — Operational cost per merchant or per transaction. Amos's industry baseline: 1 CSM per 25 merchants @ AED 7K/mo. AI-displaced model: ~7x reduction.

**Cohort** — Customers grouped by acquisition period; tracked for retention curves over time.

**Discount commitment** — A contractual obligation by the operator to fund a discount from its margin, paid through a licensed PSP. Distinguished from a Stored Value Facility because no prefunded balance is held.

**Embedded loyalty** — Loyalty mechanics surfaced inside another product's existing UX (resident app, banking app, supermarket app), not as a standalone consumer download.

**LTV** — Lifetime Value. Forecast revenue from a customer over expected tenure.

**MDR** — Merchant Discount Rate. The fee a merchant pays the acquirer/PSP per card transaction.

**MEDDPICC** — Enterprise sales qualification framework: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition.

**MSA** — Master Services Agreement. The umbrella commercial contract between an enterprise client and the loyalty operator.

**NPS** — Net Promoter Score. % promoters minus % detractors on the "would you recommend" question. Useful as a directional sentiment indicator; weak as a churn predictor on its own.

**Open finance** — Regulatory regime extending open banking beyond payments to investments, lending, insurance, loyalty data. CBUAE issued an Open Finance Regulation in 2024; KSA SAMA's Open Banking Framework is in phased rollout.

**Open-loop** — Loyalty value redeemable across an external network or convertible to cash / open-spend instruments.

**PSP** — Payment Service Provider. The licensed entity that orchestrates card and account transactions for a merchant or platform.

**RFM** — Recency / Frequency / Monetary. The three-axis customer segmentation framework.

**SaaS guarantee** — Minimum monthly fee the anchor commits to regardless of transaction volume. Floor revenue for the operator. Amos/DAMAC: $10K/mo.

**Settlement** — The clearing of cashback / reward obligations between merchant, PSP, operator, and consumer. Cadence and timing materially affect working-capital needs.

**Stored Value Facility (SVF)** — A regulated payment instrument representing prefunded value (e.g., a closed-loop wallet with a balance). Triggers CBUAE licensing in the UAE. Distinct from a discount commitment.

**Take rate** — Operator's share of merchant margin per transaction. Amos baseline: ≥10% of merchant margin.

**Tier** — Status level in a loyalty program (e.g., Bronze / Silver / Gold / Platinum) gated by spend or activity, often with decay rules.

**TPV** — Total Payment Volume. Aggregate transaction value flowing through the platform.

**White-label** — Platform delivered under the anchor's brand, not the operator's. Bilt mostly co-brands; Amos is white-labeled inside DAMAC Living.

**Zero-CAC distribution** — Anchor-app embedding model where end users arrive without per-user marketing spend. The headline number for embedded loyalty platforms.
