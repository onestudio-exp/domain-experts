# Employee Advocacy / Internal Comms — Glossary

## Domain concepts

- **Employee advocacy** — employees voluntarily sharing corporate / industry content on their personal social channels, amplifying brand voice.
- **EMV (Earned Media Value)** — calculated value of organic reach driven by employees, typically expressed in $ equivalent of paid-media spend.
- **Reach × engagement** — the standard advocacy metric pair; reach = unique people who saw, engagement = likes/comments/shares.
- **Participation rate** — % of eligible employees who shared at least once in a window. Healthy benchmark: 20-40%; weak: <10%.
- **Approval workflow** — process gating what content employees can share; ranges from **open** (no gate) to **curated** (admin-approved library) to **locked** (only pre-approved scripts).
- **Content mix** — distribution across brand / industry / personal content categories.
- **Assignment** — admin-initiated push of specific content to specific employees (vs library + voluntary share).

## Platforms & adjacent categories

- **Pure-play advocacy** — EveryoneSocial, Sociabble, GaggleAMP, PostBeyond.
- **Internal comms + advocacy** — Haiilo, Firstup, Staffbase, Workvivo.
- **Social-management bolt-on** — Hootsuite Amplify, Bambu by Sprout, Oktopost.
- **NOT advocacy:** generic social media management (Hootsuite, Sprout), PR/wire services (PRNewswire), HR comms (Workday Engage), customer-advocacy platforms (Influitive, ReferralCandy).

## KSA / MENA-specific

- **PDPL** — Personal Data Protection Law (KSA 2023). Applies to employee-data handling in advocacy.
- **SDAIA** — Saudi Data & AI Authority. Enforces KSA PDPL.
- **NCA** — National Cybersecurity Authority. Data classification rules for KSA enterprises.
- **Vision 2030** — KSA national strategy; advocacy content opportunity for KSA enterprises aligning with the narrative.
- **Hijri-aware comms** — content cadence that respects Islamic calendar (Ramadan content lull, Eid spikes, National Day surges).
- **Hierarchy / wasta** — KSA enterprise dynamics; participation patterns differ from US org charts.
