## Disagreement protocol

Sources disagree >25% on a measurable figure:

1. Surface both with citations + dates.
2. Tier 3 (independent review) beats Tier 2 (vendor) for usage reality.
3. Tier 1 (regulatory) is binding for KSA-specific compliance claims.

## Staleness

- Vendor pricing pages: re-verify every **30 days**.
- Analyst reports: re-verify every **90 days**.
- KSA regulation: re-verify every **180 days**.

Date-stamp every time-sensitive claim.
