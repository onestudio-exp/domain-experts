## Disagreement protocol

Sources disagree >25% on a measurable figure:

1. Surface **both** with citations + dates.
2. KSA regulatory primary beats vendor primary.
3. If same tier, mark `[NEEDS-RESEARCH]`.

## Staleness

- KSA regulation: re-verify every **180 days**.
- Vendor pricing/features: re-verify every **30 days**.
- Salla platform behavior: re-verify every **90 days**.

Date-stamp every time-sensitive claim. No date stamp → demote to `[NEEDS-RESEARCH]`.
