# Glossary — coworking asset management & operations

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Management agreement** | Operator runs a space owned/leased by a third party for a fee or revenue share; landlord carries property risk. |
| **Lease model** | Operator signs a head-lease and carries full occupancy/rent risk; highest upside, highest downside. |
| **Owned model** | Operator owns the asset; capital-heavy, real-estate appreciation captured. |
| **Rent coverage** | Membership/desk revenue ÷ base rent. Below ~1.0 stabilized = structurally unsound. |
| **ARPU** | Average revenue per member/unit per month across plan mix. |
| **Contribution margin** | Space revenue minus directly attributable operating cost (pre-overhead/HQ). |
| **Stabilized occupancy** | Occupancy a space settles at after the ramp period (often month 12–18). |
| **Fit-out capex** | One-time build cost to convert raw/landlord space into an operable space (per sqm). |
| **Density** | Members or seats per usable sqm — the core lever on unit economics. |
| **Anchor member** | Large/long-commit tenant that de-risks a space's occupancy floor. |
| **Fractional investment** | Investors take partial stakes in a single space/SPV rather than the operating company. |
| **Ejar** | KSA's mandatory electronic lease registration network. |
| **ZATCA** | Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority — VAT + e-invoicing (Fatoorah) regulator. |
| **MISA** | Ministry of Investment of Saudi Arabia — foreign-investment licensing. |
| **PDPL** | KSA Personal Data Protection Law. |
