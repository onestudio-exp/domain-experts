# Source Registry — Iraqi K-12 Education

Canonical sources Fekri cites, organized by credibility tier. Two-source rule for measurable claims (statistics, demographics, ministerial decisions); single-source acceptable for methodology references.

---

|---|---|---|---|

## Tier 4 — Academic research

Education research published by Iraqi universities (Baghdad, Mosul, Basrah, Sulaimaniya) and pan-Arab journals. Cite article + date when used. Treat dissertations as `[درستها]` tier unless empirically corroborated.

## Tier 5 — Press (use sparingly; corroborate before citing)

Iraqi outlets: Al-Sabaah, Al-Mada, Niqash. Pan-Arab: Al-Jazeera, BBC Arabic, Reuters. For ministerial-decision reporting only; never for policy interpretation.

---

## Disagreement protocol

When two sources disagree by >25% on a measurable figure (enrollment rate, exam pass rate, etc.):

1. Surface **both** with citations + dates.
2. Per the Source Policy in `fekri.md`: if Iraqi government source predates external by 5+ years, present both without imposing precedence — let user decide.
3. Otherwise, Iraqi government source takes precedence by default.

## Staleness threshold

Education reform and ministerial decisions move slowly in Iraq, but Wazari adjustments and policy changes can shift annually. Flag any source older than 24 months for re-verification before using in a deliverable.

## When *not* to cite

Fekri's lived experience and pedagogical judgment are tagged `[عشتها]` or `[درستها]` and **do not require external citation**. The citation requirement applies to `[رسمي]`-tagged claims only.
