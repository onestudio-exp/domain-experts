# Iraqi K-12 Education — Glossary

Domain vocabulary used by Fekri. Add entries when a term recurs across two or more sessions.

---

## Iraqi system structure

- **متوسط (G7-G9)** — intermediate stage. Three years; the gateway to track selection.
- **إعدادي (G10-G12)** — secondary stage. Three years, split across four tracks.
- **علمي (scientific track)** — most prestigious in family pressure; gateway to medicine, engineering. Heavy math/physics/chemistry/biology.
- **ادبي (literary track)** — Arabic, English, social studies, philosophy. Often perceived as "lower status" but produces strong writers, lawyers, teachers.
- **تطبيقي (applied track)** — newer; applied science, less prestigious than scientific.
- **مهني (vocational track)** — trade-focused (mechanics, electronics, accounting). Carries social stigma despite economic relevance.
- **Wazari (وزاري)** — the ministerial examination. Standardized, jurisdiction-wide. The Wazari result determines university and faculty access. Reform attempts have not removed its weight on a student's path.
- **سادس اعدادي** — final year of secondary (G12). Wazari year. No room for exploration; the decision year.
- **رابع / خامس اعدادي** — G10 and G11. Track decision lands here. Per Stance 5, this is where Iraq lacks the gap-year / exploration mechanism present in Gulf/Malaysia systems.

## Pedagogy

- **Rote learning (التلقين)** — dominant traditional method. Memorization-first, transfer-second.
- **Active learning** — situational in the Iraqi context; works for some concepts at some grade levels, fails when culturally mismatched.
- **Selective study** — student behavior pattern of focusing only on Wazari-frequent material; root cause of Stance 7 caution.
- **الاستاذ يعطيني الجواب** — the cultural expectation that the teacher provides the answer. Stance 4 frames why direct-answer AI tools accelerate, not reduce, this pattern.

## Social / cultural

- **Wazari pressure** — the family expectation that a student's Wazari result equals their economic future. Particularly heavy in علمي track. Hussein (Persona 1) lives this.
- **Dispalcement legacy (داعش 2014-2017)** — the cohort of students who lost 2-3 years of schooling. Mustafa (Persona 5) represents this segment.
- **Gulf trio (Compliance + HR + IT)** — does not apply in Iraq the way it does in Gulf enterprises. In Iraqi schools, the trio is **Family + Teacher + Wazari pressure**.
- **Diaspora ambition** — students like Noor (Persona 6) plan to attend university outside Iraq. Any design assuming "Iraqi university = endpoint" loses them.

## Iraqi dialect register

- **عراقي** — Iraqi Arabic. Several sub-dialects (Baghdadi, Basrawi, Mosuli, Najafi). Fekri defaults to a general Iraqi register comprehensible across regions.
- **شلون** — "how" (Iraqi); contrast with MSA "كيف" and Khaleeji "كيف".
- **هاي** — "this" (Iraqi); contrast with MSA "هذا/هذه".
- **عدنا** — "we have" (Iraqi); contrast with MSA "لدينا".
- **شنو** — "what" (Iraqi); contrast with MSA "ماذا/ما".

## Comparable platforms

- **Noon Academy** — Saudi-origin Arabic EdTech, scale leader; live in Iraq.
- **Almentor** — Egyptian Arabic EdTech, adult-learning roots, K-12 expansion.
- **Edraak** — Jordan-based, Queen Rania Foundation; free Arabic MOOC.
- **Madrasa.org** — UAE-based MBR Foundation; free Arabic STEM content.
- **Khan Academy Arabic** — globally-funded localization; benchmark for content quality + free access.
- **Iraqi MoE portal** — public sector e-learning; scale + reach, weak product experience.
