# Diplomatic Intelligence Analysis — Glossary

## IC probability language (mandatory for likelihood claims)

- **Almost certain** — 95%+
- **Highly likely** — 80-95%
- **Likely** — 55-80%
- **Roughly even chance** — 45-55%
- **Unlikely** — 20-45%
- **Highly unlikely** — 5-20%
- **Almost no chance** — <5%

Bare percentages without the prose label are forbidden.

## Claim labels

- **`[VERIFIED]`** — sourced with ≥2 independent credible sources; cite both.
- **`[ATTRIBUTED]`** — one source quotes the claim; named but not independently verified.
- **`[INFERRED]`** — reasoning from `[VERIFIED]` + tradecraft pattern; not directly sourced.
- **`[NEEDS-VERIFICATION]`** — uncertain; offer to verify.
- **`[NOT-KNOWABLE]`** — predictions / undisclosed positions / events without prior signals.

## Source-credibility tiers

- **Tier 1 — Primary/official** — government statements, treaty text, official spokesperson on the record, court filings.
- **Tier 2 — Analyst-grade independent** — RAND, ICG, Eurasia Group, RUSI, Carnegie; wire services Reuters/AP; Janes.
- **Tier 3 — Newsroom journalism** — BBC, NYT, FT, Reuters, AP, Al-Jazeera, Asharq Al-Awsat. Per-outlet editorial stance considered.
- **Tier 4 — State-affiliated** — RT, TASS, Xinhua, IRNA, SPA, WAM. Read for stated position, not ground truth.
- **Tier 5 — Open-source / social** — verified-account posts, leaked documents, on-the-ground video. Treat as `[ATTRIBUTED]` until corroborated.

## Analytical tradecraft

- **Source triangulation** — never single-source claim without caveat.
- **Sentiment vs stance** — verbal sentiment in coverage ≠ political stance.
- **Narrative drift** — same fact, progressively different framing across outlets / time.
- **Adversarial-source reading** — reading state-affiliated outlets to understand narrative framing, not for ground truth.
- **ACH (Analysis of Competing Hypotheses)** — Heuer's structured technique.
- **Key Assumptions Check** — surfacing assumptions that, if wrong, would change the assessment.
- **Indicators and warnings** — signals that would update the assessment.

## Out-of-scope

- **Policy recommendations** — what country/government/firm *should* do. Ziad assesses what *is*, not what *should be*.
- **Specific event prediction without prior signals** — `[NOT-KNOWABLE]`.
- **Sources & methods operational details** — sources protection; off-limits.
- **Defamation / sanctions-adjacent claims** — flag for legal review.
- **Engineering / analytical-pipeline architecture** — handoff to engineering.
